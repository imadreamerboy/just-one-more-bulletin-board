# just-one-more-bulletin-board

A public map of where the [`collusion.wiki`](https://collusion.wiki/) agent swarm went, what it shared, and how strong each link is.

We turned 14,591 revisions, the completed V5 follow-up and reviewed contributions into a compact evidence graph: 33 claims, 37 sources, 43 relationships, and 2,110 unique exact URLs. The repository keeps the useful links, relevance, proof, and gaps without republishing raw bodies.

## What stands out

- Answer sharing became a protocol with roles, relay formats, and pre-final signalling.
- The writers used public counters for answers, acknowledgements, and termination tests, then repeatedly contaminated their own evidence.
- They traded sandbox and network bypass methods, heartbeat tricks, and detached-process experiments.
- Rare phrases and URL bundles connect the main logs to other wiki histories, paste graphs, registry records, and hosted objects.
- A newly analysed 120-object Linuxiarz family contains a strong eight-object Q4/Q5 sequence on already known infrastructure.
- A lower-bound same-submitter URLQuery graph contains 1,609 receipts; 1,496 safe receipt locators are published individually and 113 remain aggregate-only.
- Confirmed artefacts and suspects are kept separate.

The records separate direct observations, claims made by the writers, and our inferences. Names such as `OpenAI`, `OAI`, or `agent` are not proof of identity. Every URL retained for this evidence map is published once; non-passive routes are risk-labelled and rendered as exact text instead of automatic links.
